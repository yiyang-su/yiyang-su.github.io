I am a Ph.D. student in [Computer Vision Lab (CVLab)](http://cvlab.cse.msu.edu) at Michigan State University advised by [Dr. Xiaoming Liu](https://www.cse.msu.edu/~liuxm/index2.html). My research is mostly focused on open-set recognition and video understanding. In summer 2025, I work as an Applied Scientist Intern at AWS AI Labs. Prior to joining MSU, I received my Bachelor of Science degrees from the University of Rochester, where I also had the opportunity to work with [Dr. Chenliang Xu](https://www.cs.rochester.edu/~cxu22/) and [Dr. Yapeng Tian](https://www.yapengtian.com/) on audio-visual sound separation.

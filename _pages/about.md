I am a Ph.D. student in [Computer Vision Lab (CVLab)](http://cvlab.cse.msu.edu) at Michigan State University advised by [Dr. Xiaoming Liu](https://cs.unc.edu/person/xiaoming-liu/). My research focuses broadly on recognition problems in computer vision. In summer 2026, I worked as an Applied Scientist Intern at Amazon AGI with Drs. [Xiang Xu](https://shownx.github.io), Zheng Zhang, [Zhuowei Li](https://lzvv123456.github.io), Linghan Xu, [Julia Xu](https://sheliaxin.github.io/Xin-Xu-Homepage/), [Zhuowen Tu](https://pages.ucsd.edu/~ztu/), [Anil Jain](https://www.cse.msu.edu/~jain/), Yifan Xing, and Tagyoung Chung. In summer 2025, I was at AWS AI Labs. Prior to joining MSU, I received my Bachelor of Science degrees from the University of Rochester, where I also had the opportunity to work with [Dr. Chenliang Xu](https://www.cs.rochester.edu/~cxu22/) and [Dr. Yapeng Tian](https://www.yapengtian.com/) on audio-visual sound separation.
